CIPHERGRID

AI evaluation · NeurIPS 2026 (accepted)

A controlled benchmark for multimodal rule inference and sequential action: can a model infer an unfamiliar symbolic world from examples, keep its rules straight, and use them to act? Even capable models struggle to integrate skills they can perform individually.

635 benchmark problems · 41.89% best model accuracy*

*As reported in the current public benchmark card; results depend on evaluation configuration.
