CCRESEARCH • DESIGN • BUILD

I'm a computer science PhD researcher at Northeastern University whose public work spans model evaluation, Transformer architectures, and human–computer interaction.

My research interests connect technical questions about AI capabilities with practical questions about system design. Published and publicly released work includes a benchmark for multimodal reasoning, research on AI for crowdwork, and human-in-the-loop approaches to technology.

My background also includes software engineering at Hewlett Packard Enterprise, independent development of simulation and AI tools, and service in the New York Army National Guard Infantry (2022–2025). These experiences inform how I build, evaluate, and communicate technical systems.
