CIPHERGRID.

From multimodal rule inference to sequential action.

A controlled benchmark for a surprisingly difficult question: can AI infer an unfamiliar symbolic world from examples, keep its rules straight, and use them to act? Across 635 puzzles, even capable models struggle to integrate skills they can perform individually.

CCRESEARCH • DESIGN • BUILD

I'm a computer science PhD researcher at Northeastern University, working across AI evaluation, Transformer architectures, and human-centered computing.

I care about how we tell whether AI systems really understand what they're doing, and how we build those systems to strengthen rather than erase human expertise. My work ranges from multimodal reasoning benchmarks to participatory research with gig workers and people navigating barriers to professional opportunity.

My background also includes software engineering at Hewlett Packard Enterprise, independent development of simulation and AI tools, and service in the New York Army National Guard Infantry (2022–2025). I bring a combination of technical engineering experience and field-informed interest in consequential work to my research.
